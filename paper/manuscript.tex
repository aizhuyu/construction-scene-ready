\title{ConstructionSceneReady: Evidence-grounded compilation and task-scene
validation for BIM-driven robotic assembly}

% Author identities are intentionally kept in title-page.tex.

\begin{abstract}
Robot-ready construction simulation requires more than converting building
geometry into a simulation format. We ask which building information actually
supports a robotic construction task in simulation, and how its absence,
error, or imprecision changes task outcomes. \systemname{} compiles IFC
source into task-activated multi-scale OpenUSD scenes and validates them
against an explicit task-scene contract with evidence-bounded repair. On 105
fault-injection cases the contract recovers all 141 findings without false
positives and repairs 97.1\% to pristine equivalence; an agent constrained to
the same whitelist preserves it, while an unconstrained agent produces
incorrect-but-passing scenes in 22.7\%. Paired humanoid episodes show
symbolic and referential defects are discretely fatal and clearance is the
most sensitive continuous property. The 12\,mm reward criterion also masks
anchor-relative interface errors: geometrically valid insertion collapses to
2.0\% at a 10\,mm hole-coordinate error while reward success stays
${\sim}80\%$, a blind spot the contract closes with two independently
verified rules.
\end{abstract}
