\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper frames robot-ready construction simulation as a scene compilation
and validation problem rather than a file-conversion problem. It proposes a
multi-scale IFC-to-OpenUSD pipeline, a construction task-scene contract, and a
deterministically grounded repair process. On a frozen fault-injection
partition the contract validator recovered every expected finding without
false positives and repaired 97.1\% of cases to pristine-scene equivalence,
escalating exactly those cases whose repair would exceed the available
engineering evidence. Grounding a language-model agent in the contract
preserved this reliability (96.8\% valid, zero incorrect repairs), whereas the
same model without the contract produced validator-passing but incorrect
scenes in nearly a quarter of episodes---direct evidence that scene-level
engineering authority, not model capability, is the binding constraint on
agentic scene repair.

The humanoid defect benchmark closes the loop between scene models and task
outcomes. Measured over 10{,}600 paired insertion episodes, defects in symbolic
and referential features---semantic identity, task references, construction
states---were discretely fatal; and the 12\,mm reward criterion was shown to
mask anchor-relative interface errors: a 10\,mm hole-coordinate error left
reward success at 79.0\% while geometric-valid insertion collapsed to 2.0\%,
whereas the same 10\,mm absolute member shift stayed largely correctable
(geometric-valid 41.5\%). Cross-checking these outcomes against the validator
showed perfect specificity but 40\% sensitivity at the condition level: the
contract sees every catastrophic defect family yet remains blind to
sub-tolerance geometric and interface fidelity, which motivates the two
candidate rules registered in this work and, more broadly, positions runtime
task measurement as a calibration instrument for scene-readiness contracts.

The results are bounded by a single executor, a single assembly task, a
kinematic grasp scaffold, and simulation-only evidence; extending the defect
benchmark to contact-rich stages and additional embodiments, and completing
the whole-body task levels, is the natural next step. The framework's
artefacts---the frozen partition, registered scripts, and per-episode
ledgers---are intended to make every number in this paper regenerable.
