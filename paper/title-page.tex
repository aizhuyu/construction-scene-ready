\title{ConstructionSceneReady: Evidence-grounded compilation and task-scene
validation for BIM-driven robotic assembly}

\author[aff1]{Zhu Aiyu\corref{cor1}}
\ead{zhu.aiyu@yango.edu.cn}

\begin{abstract}
Robot-ready construction simulation requires more than converting building
geometry into a simulation format. We ask which building information actually
supports a robotic construction task in simulation, and how its absence,
error, or imprecision changes task outcomes. ConstructionSceneReady compiles
IFC source into task-activated multi-scale OpenUSD scenes and validates them
against an explicit task-scene contract with evidence-bounded repair. On 105
fault-injection cases the contract recovers all 141 findings without false
positives and repairs 97.1\% of scenes to pristine equivalence; an agent
constrained to the same whitelist preserves this reliability, while an
unconstrained agent produces validator-passing but incorrect scenes in
22.7\%. Paired humanoid insertion episodes show symbolic and referential
defects are discretely fatal, interface clearance is the most sensitive
continuous property, and absolute member-pose errors are absorbed by
closed-loop perception. Crossing these outcomes with the contract yields
perfect specificity but limited geometric sensitivity, motivating two
contract rules verified on an independent test set.
\end{abstract}
